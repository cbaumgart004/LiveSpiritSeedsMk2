Commit 47ceb42 made the home splash (`.splash--lap`) stack in flow below 820px and ignore its phone
arrangement (`data-eotm-phone='free'`, `phoneParts` and `phoneHeight` in `_layout`). The reason:
the arrangement places parts on a canvas of fixed height (`aspect-ratio: 100 / var(--frame-qh)`,
`styles/frame.css`), so when the stacked tagline text ran taller than the owner's placement allowed,
the artwork was clipped or overlapped. The owner wants edits made in Arrange on a phone to apply again.

Two parts, site and console together:

1. **Phone arrangements apply again.** Remove the flow override in `layout.css` (the rule on
   `.splash--lap[data-eotm-phone='free']` below 820px) once a placement can no longer clip its own
   contents: the canvas grows to its tallest part rather than holding a fixed height.
2. **A choice for a confirmed phone layout**, owner-facing: "Allow text to grow or shrink with the
   screen size", or similar. On: placed text scales with the screen width (container units, as
   `.eotm-design` already sizes its text). Off: text keeps the size the owner set in Arrange. It
   needs a schema field (the console's `_layout`, or a per-section field in
   `console/schema/sites/spiritseeds.json`) and the console's Arrange to offer it.

**Unverified:** whether 640px to 819px (between a phone and a computer) should use the phone
arrangement or the desktop one.
